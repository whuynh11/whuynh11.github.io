Myself

I’m a senior product designer who specializes in design systems, scalable UX, and cross-functional collaboration. Over the last few years, I’ve led design system initiatives across startups and enterprise teams—helping product and engineering move faster with fewer trade-offs.

I’ve designed experiences that support mental health, improve financial literacy, and increase user engagement across mobile and web platforms. My work has driven measurable impact—like reducing tech/design debt by 30%, boosting adoption rates, and increasing retention through thoughtful UX.
