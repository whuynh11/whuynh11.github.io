About Me

I’m a Senior Product Designer who blends systems thinking with product growth, shipping experiences that move key metrics and building the system layer that helps teams scale with confidence.

I’ve spent 8+ years shipping B2B and B2C experiences across web + mobile, with a focus on activation, engagement loops, and retention. My hybrid background (product-minded, technically fluent) helps me bridge PM and engineering, turning ambiguous problems into clear experiments and production-ready solutions.

I build the design system foundations that make wins repeatable: components, tokens, accessibility standards, and lightweight governance that reduce rework and increase velocity. Recently, I’ve been leveraging AI to tighten the cycle from insight → hypothesis → prototype → QA, so teams can move faster without lowering the quality bar.
